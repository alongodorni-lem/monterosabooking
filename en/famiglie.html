FAQ

Frequently asked questions for families

Is Macugnaga suitable as a mountain destination with children?

Yes: easy routes, a welcoming village and guided experiences. Ideal among the family mountains of Monte Rosa, including for day trips from Milan, Lake Maggiore, Varese and Novara.

Which family experiences can you book?

Nature and play, Walser House Museum, gold mine (including with a pushchair), gold panning and other activities. See Experiences.

How do you organise a mountain weekend with children?

Stay in Macugnaga, book one or two experiences online and combine with village walks. Practical guide on Weekend.
